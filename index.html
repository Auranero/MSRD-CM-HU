About the Medical Scientific Research Department (MSRD)

The Medical Scientific Research Department (MSRD) was established at the College of Medicine, Hebron University, to govern, standardize, and catalyze medical scientific research. MSRD acts as both an academic advisory body and the official institutional repository for monitoring peer-reviewed scientific publications originating from faculty members, clinical supervisors, and medical students.

Mission Statement

To foster an ethically grounded, internationally recognized research ecosystem within the College of Medicine by supporting medical students and faculty in producing high-impact clinical and biomedical publications that address regional and global health challenges.

Strategic Vision

To position Hebron University College of Medicine as a premier hub for evidence-based medical research in Palestine, characterized by student-led research integration, high Q1/Q2 journal publication rates, and robust international collaborative networks.

Undergraduate Medical Research

Student Research Hub & Roadmap

The College of Medicine encourages early research engagement. Over 60% of MSRD publication records involve medical student co-authors or student lead investigators.

Step 1: Mentor Match

Form a team and select a faculty supervisor from MSRD directory.

Step 2: IRB Clearance

Draft protocol and submit for institutional ethics review.

Step 3: Data & Stats

Collect data, conduct statistical analysis with MSRD assistance.

Step 4: Manuscript

Write following ICMJE authorship guidelines and target Q1/Q2 journal.

Step 5: MSRD Listing

Submit published article proof for official observatory verification.

Ethics & Research Governance

IRB Submission

Select the appropriate IRB submission pathway before starting data collection. The forms below are separate and should be completed according to the research category.

IRB for Graduation Project (Thesis)

Graduation Project research is the research proposal required for the course M-1594 before starting the data collection process. The PI and all Researchers must be from the College of Medicine at Hebron University.

IRB for External Research

External Research is research in which the Principal Investigator (PI) is not affiliated with the College of Medicine at Hebron University.

IRB for Internal Research

Internal Research is research in which the Principal Investigator (PI) is affiliated with the College of Medicine at Hebron University.

Important: Data collection must not begin before the required ethical/IRB approval has been obtained. Use the correct submission category for your study.
